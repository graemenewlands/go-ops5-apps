How it works under the Rete Algorithm: There are zero imperative for loops iterating over the grid. Instead, each generation runs through 5 declarative stages:
  1. Emit Votes: Live cells join with 8 directional delta vectors to assert neighbor candidate votes with toroidal modulo wrapping.
  2. Tally Votes: An accumulator pattern consumes votes and tallies them per coordinate.
  3. Evaluate Conway Rules: Relational pattern tests trigger birth (count=3) and survival (count=2,3).
  4. Clear & Promote: Retracts old cells and promotes next-cell facts.
  5. Quiescence: Execution pauses naturally when no rules match, waiting for the next tick.
